🌐 Ghost Tab

A hidden side-panel browser that slides into any webpage. Browse, search, or reference content without ever leaving your current tab.

Show side tab Display the trigger on page edges

How It Works

  1. Look for the slim bookmark tab on the right edge of any website you visit.
  2. Click the button to slide open the side-panel browser window.
  3. Type a URL or search term in the input bar and press Enter.
  4. To hide it instantly, click × or double-click anywhere on the page.
